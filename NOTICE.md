`vault/` is original research writing. It describes other games' designs and cites its sources
at the end of each file. It contains none of those games' assets, names used as branding, or
content, and nothing built with this toolkit should copy them.

## The trends tracker

`tools/trends` reads public, unauthenticated Roblox web endpoints. They are unofficial and
subject to Roblox's terms of use. What it collects is stored on your machine only, in
`private/trends/`; the tracker creates that folder on its first run.
