## Look
- Font: Builder Sans <weight>; sizes for HUD numbers, buttons, body (roblox-ui `references/ui-ux.md`)
- Panels: color, transparency, `UICorner` radius, `UIStroke`
- Buttons: idle, pressed, disabled looks; press feedback (roblox-vfx `references/game-feel.md`)
- Icons: one set, one style; currency icon next to every price and counter
