# Steal a ___ (base-raiding idle)

## In one line

Each player owns a base in a small server (4–8 players). Units placed in the base earn cash every
second. You get better units by buying them, grabbing them from guarded spawns, or stealing them
from other bases and running them home. You defend your own base with a timed lock, gear and
traps, and rebirth for permanent multipliers. The two defining games are
Steal a Brainrot (case study: steal-a-brainrot) (2025) and
Steal An Egg (case study: steal-an-egg), which was #1 on Roblox with 877K players
(data, 2026-10-22).

## Core loop

**Moment to moment (5–60 s)**
- Go to a source: a conveyor of units for sale (Steal a Brainrot) or a guarded nest in a biome
  (Steal An Egg). Pay, or grab and run (press, Wikipedia/PC Gamer 2025-09; community, games.gg
  2026-08-29).
- Carry the unit home. Carrying is the vulnerable state. A hit makes the carrier drop it and the
  unit returns to its slot, and in Steal a Brainrot a carrier can't use gear (community, Fiction
  Horizon 2026-09-18). A steal only counts when the unit reaches your base (community, 2026-08).
- Collect the cash your base has earned. Chase and hit anyone who grabs from your base.

**Session (10–20 min)**
- Fill every slot, then replace the weakest unit with a better one. Save up for the next tier.
- React to rare spawns. Steal An Egg runs rare eggs on a ~5-minute cycle and announces Secret
  eggs in chat (community, games.gg 2026-08-29).
- Lock your base before you walk away from it, and raid bases whose lock has just run out.
- Join the recurring event. Steal An Egg runs a boss, drone or race event every 30 minutes
  (community, rblxguide 2026-10).

**Long term (days to weeks)**
- **Rebirth.** Steal a Brainrot's rebirths each need a cash total plus specific units. Each one
  resets cash and units, grants a permanent bonus and adds 10 s to the lock (community guides,
  2025–2026, unverified).
- **Rarity ladder plus mutations.** Steal An Egg lists 10 pet tiers, Common to Divine (community,
  2026-10-18). Mutations multiply income: in Steal a Brainrot, Gold 1.25×, Diamond 1.5×, Rainbow
  10× per fan guides (community, unverified).
- **Stat gates.** Steal An Egg gates biomes behind a Speed stat trained on a treadmill. Its Angels
  vs Demons biome needs 20B Speed (press, StudioKrew 2026-10-01).
- **Collection.** An index pays a reward the first time you discover each unit (community,
  2026-08-29).
- **Weekly content.** Both leaders ship new units and an admin event every week (see
  [Why players come back](#why-players-come-back)).

## Main verbs

Buy, grab, carry, steal, deliver, lock, hit, collect, place, upgrade, rebirth. Variants add
hatch (Steal An Egg), train a stat (Speed, Grip), fuse (3 copies → 1 higher tier) and break
(Break and Steal an Egg).

## Why players come back

- **The loop explains itself.** Both leaders' descriptions fit the whole game in five or six
  bullets, such as "Buy… Steal… Generate money… Rebirth" (data, game descriptions 2026-10-22).
  A new player understands it from watching one steal. (inferred)
- **Social conflict.** Press compare it to capture the flag (press, GamesRadar+ 2025-08-27).
  Losing a unit to a named rival creates a revenge goal. (inferred)
- **Clips.** Strategy clips and children crying over stolen units drew tens of millions of
  TikTok/YouTube views (press, Game Rant 2025-07-26). Clips bring new players. See
  [social-and-virality](../fundamentals/social-and-virality.md).
- **Visible wealth.** Your base shows your best units to the whole server. (inferred)
- **Weekly heartbeat.** Steal a Brainrot was on its 44th update by about 2026-03, with weekly
  drops and regular admin events (community, RoWatcher). Steal An Egg shipped four Saturday
  updates in a row in 2026-09 (press, StudioKrew 2026-10-01). Fan guides put Steal a Brainrot's
  admin events on Saturdays and Tuesdays, 30–45 min each (community, unverified).
- **Something earns while you're away.** Several entries pay offline income, such as "Your
  Animals earns Cash offline!" (data, Don't Steal My Eggs! description 2026-10-22). See
  [idle-offline-income](../patterns/idle-offline-income.md).

## What separates top games from clones

1. **A second proven loop under the stealing.** Steal An Egg added egg hatching, stat training
   and AI guardians, and passed Steal a Brainrot 14 months after it launched (data + inferred).
   See [trend-lifecycle](../fundamentals/trend-lifecycle.md).
2. **PvE sources as well as PvP.** In Steal An Egg most stealing is from AI guardians. Players
   rob each other mainly while a rival is carrying or raiding (community, 2026-08). A new player
   can progress in a server full of stronger players. (inferred)
3. **Ladder depth.** Ten rarity tiers plus sizes, mutations and fusion give months of targets
   (community, 2026-10). Clones often stop at 4–5 tiers. (inferred)
4. **Weekly updates plus a scheduled event**, every week (see above).
5. **Original content and names.** Steal a Brainrot's owners have filed at least four lawsuits
   against imitators since 2025-11 (press, Bloomberg 2026-03-24).

## Session shape

- **Typical session:** ~15 min average playtime for Steal An Egg (press, Player.One citing
  Rolimon's, 2026-09-09). That's the 13–18 min tier in [retention](../fundamentals/retention.md),
  where median D1 is 10.2% (press, GameAnalytics 2025).
- **First 60 s (inferred targets):** spawn inside your own base with one free starter unit
  already earning. An arrow points to the source. The first purchase or grab is affordable within
  30 s, and income visibly ticks up when the unit is placed.
- **First 10 min (inferred targets):** 4–6 slots filled, first steal attempt (prompted when a
  rival's lock runs out), first lock pressed, first rarer unit seen on the conveyor or spawn,
  and a "next tier costs X" goal on screen.
- **Day 7 (inferred targets):** 1–3 rebirths, a unit from tier 5+, knows the event times, has a
  rival or a friend group they play with.

## Social hooks

- **Small servers make rivals personal.** Steal An Egg runs 7 per server, Steal a Brainrot 8,
  Don't Steal My Eggs! 4 (data, 2026-10-22). Across Roblox's 83 charted Tycoon-subgenre games
  the median server size is 6 (data, 2026-10-22).
- **Server announcements** for rare spawns and big steals make one player's luck everyone's
  event. (inferred)
- **Friends join the same server** to team-raid or guard. That counts toward intentional co-play
  days, a discovery signal (official, 2026-10). See
  [discovery-algorithm](../fundamentals/discovery-algorithm.md).
- **Co-op flip.** Don't Steal My Eggs! has players catch enemies to protect their eggs (data,
  description 2026-10-22). Defending together is a spin on the same base.
- **Events are shared.** Steal a Brainrot's mock "admin war" with Grow a Garden on 2025-08-23
  pushed both games to records (press, PocketGamer.biz 2025-08-27).

## Monetization that works here

| Product | Example | Price (dated) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2× cash pass | Steal An Egg X2 Money | 399 R$ (press, StudioKrew, checked 2026-09-05) | Core pass for any income game |
| 2× timer speed pass | Steal An Egg X2 Growth Speed | 467 R$ (press, same) | Speeds hatching for good |
| Per-use timer skip | Instant Hatch ~9 R$/egg, Grow All Eggs ~160 R$ | (community, 2026-08-27, unverified) | Repeatable, but see Don't |
| Robux-only unit/egg | Monster Egg → Luminous Egg → Extinction Egg | (community, rblxguide 2026-10) | Rotated with updates |
| Weekly-capped consumable | Shooting Stars, max 5/week | (community, rblxguide 2026-10) | Cap limits whale spend |
| Gear and traps | Steal a Brainrot sells gear for cash or Robux | (press, Game Rant 2025-07-26) | Sell defense/convenience |
| Lock time | Steal a Brainrot VIP reportedly +10 s lock | (community, unverified) | Sells safety, not theft |
| Pass gifting | Steal An Egg added it in Update 4 | (press, StudioKrew 2026-10-01) | Social spending |

- Steal An Egg had no codes system as of 2026-10 (press, PCGamesN and PocketGamer 2026-10).
- Spend days per user is a discovery signal, so cheap repeatable products help more than one
  big pass (official, 2026-10; see [discovery-algorithm](../fundamentals/discovery-algorithm.md)).
- Steal a Brainrot was criticized for selling the best items and "server administrator"
  abilities (press, Polygon 2025-07-25). Treat paid power as a reputation cost.
- Roblox says players dislike timers that block play and can be skipped with Robux (official,
  2026-10). Steal An Egg sells egg-timer skips anyway. Sell a permanent speed pass instead.
  (inferred)

## Common failure modes

- **Strong players farm new ones.** A server of rebirthed players empties a new player's base
  and they quit. Fix it with PvE sources, steal targets weighted toward similar income, and a
  short spawn lock. (inferred)
- **Scripts and auto-farmers.** Automated cash collection and stealing were common in Steal a
  Brainrot (press, Game Rant 2025-07-26). Script lists exist for Steal An Egg (community,
  MobileMatters 2026). Teleport-delivery ("instant steal") is the worst case because it turns
  the whole loop off. (inferred) See
  [security](../../.claude/skills/roblox-code/references/security.md).
- **Theft from players who aren't there.** Bases should exist only while the owner is in the
  server. A steal while offline is the "punishing absence" pattern
  [retention](../fundamentals/retention.md) rejects. (inferred)
- **Reward-for-watching features.** Steal An Egg's treadmill showed a short-video feed. The game
  was offline for ~3–4 h on 2026-08-24/25 and came back without it (press, allthings.how
  2026-08). Since 2026-08-25, games that reward ongoing viewing of an autoplaying or scrolling
  media feed are excluded from Roblox Kids and Select (official, DevForum 2026-08-25).
- **Reskin only.** Same title pattern plus same visuals is what discovery deprioritizes
  (official, 2026-10). See [trend-lifecycle](../fundamentals/trend-lifecycle.md).
- **Runaway inflation.** If income grows faster than prices, the top tier arrives in a day and
  rebirth stops mattering. (inferred)
- **No weekly update.** Wave games that stop updating fade within weeks (community, RoWatcher,
  2026).
- **Lag from too many moving units.** 8 bases × 10–20 animated units on a phone. See
  [performance-and-devices](../../.claude/skills/roblox-map/references/performance-and-devices.md). (inferred)

## Scope for a solo dev

**MVP (must ship)** (inferred)
- 6 plots per server, assigned on join and cleared on leave. 10 unit slots per plot.
- One source: a conveyor or a spawn field with weighted rarity rolls (6 tiers).
- Units earn cash/s and pay out through a collect pad, computed server-side from timestamps.
- Stealing: hold a prompt on a rival's unit, carry it at reduced speed, deliver it to your plot.
- Defense: lock button (60 s) inside the base, plus a bat whose hit drops the carried unit.
- Rebirth (cash + one unit requirement, ×1.5 income each), an index, autosave.
- Shop UI, 2 passes (2× cash, +1 slot or VIP lock time), 2 cheap products.

**Build order** (one developer with Claude Code; rough weeks, inferred)

| Week | Build |
|---|---|
| 1 | Plots, spawner, buy, place, income, collect, [player-data](../patterns/player-data.md) save |
| 2 | Steal, carry, deliver, lock, bat; server validation; playtest with 4 clients in Studio MCP |
| 3 | Rarity tiers and mutations, rebirth, index, shop and passes, FTUE arrow and goals |
| 4 | Mobile UI, performance pass, one admin-style event, thumbnails/icon, launch |
| 5+ | Weekly drop: 4–8 new units plus one event per week |

**No artist**
- Units as simple primitive or generated models (Studio MCP `generate_mesh` /
  `generate_procedural_model`) with strong silhouettes. Rarity reads as color, size, outline and
  particles. Mutations are material and color swaps. (inferred)
- Never import another game's characters or meme figures. Original names only.

**Cut from v1:** trading, fusion, mounts, multiple biomes, boss events, traps beyond one, Robux
gear, cross-server events. Add them as weekly updates if D1 holds. (inferred)

- Steal An Egg (case study: steal-an-egg): steal from guarded nests, hatch, train Speed;
  #1 on Roblox, 877K (data, 2026-10-22).
- Steal a Brainrot (case study: steal-a-brainrot): the original conveyor + steal + lock
  loop; 25.4M peak (press, 2025-10), 104K now (data, 2026-10-22).
- Break and Steal an Egg: adds a pickaxe "break" step and guards; 69K, 97% likes (data,

  2026-10-22).

## Build checklist for agents

Numbers are starting values to tune unless tagged otherwise. (inferred)

### Do

- Set server size to 6–8 (leaders: 7–8, data 2026-10-22). One plot per player slot.
- Give each plot 8–12 slots at start, +1–2 per rebirth or upgrade.
- Price units by payback time: price = income/s × payback. Payback 30–60 s for the lowest tier,
  rising to 10–20 min for the top tier.
- Make each rarity tier earn 3–5× the tier below. Use 6–10 tiers. Spawn weights roughly halve to
  quarter per tier (e.g. 50/25/12/7/4/1.5/0.5%).
- Grow repeated-purchase costs by ×1.07–1.15 per item owned (AdVenture Capitalist used 1.07,
  press, Kongregate 2016). See [upgrade-trees](../patterns/upgrade-trees.md).
- Lock: 30 s automatically on join, 60 s when pressed, +10 s per rebirth (matches community
  reports for Steal a Brainrot, unverified). The button must be inside the base, so the owner
  has to come back to re-lock.
- Steal: 0.5–1.5 s hold to grab, carrier speed −20–30%, no gear while carrying, a hit returns
  the unit to its slot.
- Give 10–15 s of steal protection after a player spawns or rejoins.
- Rebirth: ×1.5–2 income per rebirth, needs cash plus one named unit, keeps the index.
- Compute income server-side from `os.time()` timestamps. Pay offline income on join, capped
  at 2–8 h.
- Validate every steal on the server: thief near the unit at grab, carry state set, plot not
  locked, delivery distance to own plot, travel time ≥ distance / max speed.
- Treat `ProximityPrompt.Triggered` as a request. Re-check distance and lock state on the
  server, since the server doesn't enforce `MaxActivationDistance` (official, 2026-10).
- Grant Robux items only in `MarketplaceService.ProcessReceipt`; check passes with
  `MarketplaceService:UserOwnsGamePassAsync`.
- Save the base immediately after a steal, purchase or rebirth, and use session locking
  ([player-data](../patterns/player-data.md)).
- Ship one weekly update with new units, plus one scheduled event per week from launch.
- Add at least one PvE source so a new player can progress without stealing.

### Don't

- Don't let anyone steal from a player who has left the server.
- Don't sell lock-breaking, guaranteed steals, admin commands or damage to other players for
  Robux.
- Don't sell Robux skips for timers that gate the core loop. Sell a permanent 2× speed pass.
- Don't reward watching any autoplaying or scrolling media feed (Roblox Kids/Select rule,
  official 2026-08-25).
- Don't trust the client for income, carry state, positions at delivery or prices.
- Don't use another game's unit names, meme characters, art, sounds or title plus visuals.
- Don't let the top tier cost less than 1 hour of a mid-game player's income.
- Don't run 20+ animated units per base on mobile without LOD or animation culling.
- Don't make servers larger than 10. Rivals stop being personal and plots get far apart.
- Don't ship without a weekly update plan.
