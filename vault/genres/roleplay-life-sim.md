# Roleplay and life sim

## In one line

You build a stage, not a game: a town, roles, homes, vehicles, outfits, props and tools that
start scenes between players. The players write the stories, and other players are the
content. The genre is the third-biggest on Roblox, and one game holds most of it.

Related files: Brookhaven (case study: brookhaven) (open sandbox),
Adopt Me! (case study: adopt-me) (care loop, collection, trading),
[social-hangout](social-hangout.md) (the 16+ label this genre must avoid),
[pets-companions](../patterns/pets-companions.md), [trading](../patterns/trading.md),
[inventory](../patterns/inventory.md), [private-servers](../patterns/private-servers.md),
[fashion-avatar](fashion-avatar.md), [job-sims](job-sims.md).

## Two models

| | Open sandbox | Progression roleplay |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Brookhaven, Berry Avenue, LifeTogether, Dreamville | Adopt Me!, Welcome to Bloxburg |
| Money | No currency. Everything free is available at once (community, Brookhaven wiki 2026) | Currency from care tasks or jobs; houses and furniture are bought (community, wikis 2026) |
| Long-term hook | New houses, props and vehicles every week; private-server communities | Collection, house building, trading |
| Spend | Passes for extra house types, vehicles, VIP | Passes plus currency, eggs, subscriptions |

Pick one on purpose. A sandbox has no content gate, so it needs a steady stream of new
things. A progression game needs an economy, saving and anti-exploit work from day 1
(inferred).

## Core loop

- **Moment-to-moment (5–30 s):** pick an outfit or role → grab a tool or vehicle → use it
  *on* someone (cuff them, treat them, serve them food) → they react → the scene moves on.
- **Session (15–30 min):** spawn → claim a house in 1–2 taps → change outfit → drive to a
  location (school, hospital, police station) → join or start a scene → switch role when
  bored.
- **Long-term (weeks):**
  - Sandbox: try each week's new house, vehicle and props, and join a private-server group.
  - Progression: earn currency → furnish the house → raise and collect pets → trade.

Adopt Me's care tasks send players around the map. A pet gets "School", "Camping" or "Sick",
and the owner must go to that place for a while to clear it (community, Adopt Me wiki 2026).
Needs that point at locations spread players across the town and create chance meetings
(inferred).

## Main verbs

Be (outfit, role, morph), live (claim and decorate a home), drive, use a tool on another
player, place props, host (private server, party), care (pets, babies), show off, trade.
In a pure sandbox only the first six exist (inferred).

## Why players come back

- **Friends and cast.** A scene needs other people. Brookhaven ranked #15 on the Fun with
  Friends chart (data, 2026-10-22). Co-play days are a ranking signal
  ([social-and-virality](../fundamentals/social-and-virality.md)).
- **Something new every week.** Brookhaven has shipped weekly updates, usually Fridays at
  6 PM EST, since the Voldex takeover in 2025-02. October 2026 alone brought a military base
  and a free villa (10-02), a hospital refresh (10-09) and a wellness estate (10-16)
  (community, Brookhaven wiki changelog).
- **Seasonal collect events.** Brookhaven's Halloween event has players collect Candy Corn
  to unlock tools, props, vehicles and a house (official game description, 2026-10-24).
- **Daily rewards in progression games.** Adopt Me pays 25, 50, 100, 200, then 300 Bucks over
  a 5-day cycle, plus a Star each day (community, Adopt Me wiki 2026). See
  [daily-rewards-and-streaks](../patterns/daily-rewards-and-streaks.md).
- **Weekly live events.** Adopt Me has run "Admin Abuse" every Saturday at 9 a.m. Pacific
  since 2025-11-01, with 3× Bucks and pet XP (community, Adopt Me wiki 2026).

## What separates top games from clones

1. **Tools that act on other players.** Brookhaven's 2026-08-21 prison update added
   handcuffs that escort another player and a scanner wand that detects weapons (community,
   wiki). A prop you can only look at starts no scene (inferred).
2. **Zero friction.** Brookhaven has no tutorial, no levels and no required goals. Every
   menu teaches itself with a one-time tip (community, wiki 2026).
3. **Volume and cadence.** 5–20 new props, tools or vehicles a week, with a new house most
   weeks (community, wiki changelog 2026). Clones launch with one town and stop.
4. **Roles you can read at a glance.** Uniforms, role tools and name colors. Adopt Me
   colors chat names by role: parents red, babies blue (community, wiki).
5. **Owner moderation in private servers.** Brookhaven's private servers add prop saving,
   server music and announcements, remove/lock props, check prop owners, set admins,
   kick/temp-ban, and time, weather and theme controls (official game description,
   2026-10-24). Organized groups police their own scenes (inferred).
6. **Prop limits that protect phones.** Brookhaven caps props per player at 45 in public
   servers (50 with VIP) and lets private servers buy up to 2,000 per server (community,
   wiki 2026-07-31).
7. **Brand deals at the top.** Jurassic World (2026-09) and Minions (2026-06) events in
   Brookhaven (community, wiki). Not available to a solo dev; don't plan for them.

## Session shape

- Average playtime: Brookhaven 17.1 min, Adopt Me 25.8 min (community, Rolimons, read
  2026-10-24). Servers hold 30 and 35 players (data, 2026-10-22).
- **First 60 seconds (inferred):** spawn in the town center, a "claim a house" button on
  screen, a free car within 30 s, an outfit menu one tap away.
- **First 10 minutes (inferred):** a house claimed and changed once, one role picked, one
  tool used on another player, one scene seen nearby.
- **Day 7 (inferred):** a favorite house variant, the map learned, a friend group, and
  maybe a private server.

## Social hooks

- **Casting:** families, police and robbers, hospital, school, restaurant. Give each a
  building and 3–5 role tools (inferred).
- **Private servers** for organized roleplay groups; see
  [private-servers](../patterns/private-servers.md).
- **Chat is often off.** Age-banded chat hides many messages (official, 2026-01; see
  [audience](../fundamentals/audience.md)). Adopt Me added trade backpack viewing and
  "suggest item" buttons on 2026-01-14 in response (community, wiki). Roleplay must work
  through emotes, tools, role tags and system messages.
- **Story videos and clips.** Skits filmed in roleplay towns feed TikTok and YouTube
  (inferred). Freecam (Shift+P) and private servers make them easier to film.

## Safety and moderation: the rules that decide reach

Most players here are young. These rules decide whether the game can reach under-16s at all.

- **Roleplay is not a social hangout.** Games whose primary theme is roleplay are excluded
  from the 16+ hangout category. The roleplay must be central: "adopting a new role and using
  in-game items to play your role" (official, content maturity docs 2026-10).
- **Proof it works:** Brookhaven, Adopt Me!, Berry Avenue and Welcome to Bloxburg are all
  labeled **Minimal** (official, game-page labels read 2026-10-24). That makes them eligible
  for Kids and Select accounts.
- **Words matter.** Calling the game a hangout in its title or description classifies it as
  one (official). Berry Avenue's description says "roleplay, hang out and explore" and it
  is still Minimal (official label, 2026-10-24). Don't rely on that leniency (inferred).
- **Private spaces.** Since 2025-08-15, games that mainly take place in bedrooms or bathrooms,
  or in adult venues such as bars and clubs, are ID-verified 17+. Beds or bathroom assets in
  dollhouses or simulations aren't automatically covered; overall design decides
  (official, newsroom 2025-08-15). Current docs put hangouts with private spaces at
  age-verified 18+ (official, 2026-10).
- **Romance is Restricted (18+),** even in the title (official). Community Standards ban
  pursuing romantic relationships and "suggestive game settings" (official). So no dating,
  weddings, couple emotes or boyfriend/girlfriend roles.
- **Roblox auto-removes servers** with a high volume of violative behavior, then works with
  the developer to prevent it (official, 2025-08-15). Repeat takedowns mean a design problem.
- **Free-form creation is 16+.** No drawing, spray paint, whiteboards or pixel grids. Building
  from 3D props and assembling outfits are excluded (official).
- **Avatars:** the questionnaire doesn't cover the clothing and accessories players bring
  (official). Asset moderation still applies.
- **Violence:** Brookhaven sells a prison pass with a pistol and tear gas launcher and is still
  Minimal (community, wiki 2026-08; official label 2026-10-24). Keep weapons non-lethal and
  bloodless: stun, ragdoll, jail (inferred).

- **Paid houses are variants.** About 27% of Brookhaven's houses are paid. They are mostly
  variants of free ones and hold no exclusive secrets (community, wiki 2026).
- **New pass with new content.** Brookhaven created its House Pets and Prison Landmark passes
  in 2026-07 (official listing dates), ahead of the updates that used them on 2026-07-31 and
  2026-08-21 (community, wiki changelog).
- **Try before you buy.** Brookhaven's paid event items sit in areas players can enter and
  try first (community, wiki).
- **Rewarded ads as a free path.** Players can watch an ad to use a pass feature for 10 or 30
  minutes (community, wiki). Flow and limits are in
  [monetization](../fundamentals/monetization.md#rewarded-video-ads).
- **Tiny consumables:** Brookhaven sells special fireworks at 5 Robux per 10 uses (community,
  wiki 2026-07).
- **Avatar item commissions:** catalog items sold in-game pay the game owner 40% (official).
  Outfit menus are a natural place for them.
- **Private servers and subscriptions:** Plus members join paid private servers free, and you
  earn up to 100 Robux per active Plus member (official, 2026-04). Adopt Me sells a 299-Robux
  monthly subscription (community, wiki); see [monetization](../fundamentals/monetization.md).

## Common failure modes

- **Empty town.** Roleplay needs density. A 30-player map with 6 people in it feels dead
  (inferred). Start with a compact map and 12–16 max players.
- **A pretty map with no tools.** Nothing to do *to* each other, so nothing happens.
- **Paywalled core.** Paid-only houses or cars push away the young majority who can't buy
  (inferred; see [audience](../fundamentals/audience.md)).
- **The content treadmill.** A sandbox lives on weekly drops. Without a plan for 10+ new items
  a week, players move on (inferred).
- **Free-model towns.** Creator Store buildings and cars often carry hidden scripts and look
  like every other clone (inferred; see
  [security](../../.claude/skills/roblox-code/references/security.md)).
- **Safety slips:** unfiltered sign text, lockable single-person rooms, romance props, or
  "hangout" in the description. Each one can move the label to 16+ or 18+.
- **Phone lag:** 30 players each placing dozens of props. Cap props per player and per server.

## Saturation & opportunity

One snapshot, so these numbers show size, not growth (data, 2026-10-22).

- **Roleplay & Avatar Sim** is the third-biggest genre: 849K players, 12.1% share, 70 charted
  games (data).
- **Life subgenre:** 494K players across 27 games. Brookhaven alone has 322K, about 65% of it
  (inferred: 322 ÷ 494). The other 26 share about 172K (inferred).
- **The `roleplay` name theme** is labeled *mixed*: 37 games, 694K players, median 2.9K,
  Brookhaven 46% (data).
- **Next tier:** Berry Avenue 43K, LifeTogether 27K, Dreamville 19K (created 2024-09-07, 95%
  likes), Welcome to Bloxburg 18.7K, Dubai RP 11K (created 2024-11-09), Emergency Hamburg
  9.7K, NewSmith 6.7K, San Diego Roleplay 6.6K (created 2026-03-08) (data).
- **Single-setting roleplay** exists at small scale: Maple Hospital 4.3K, Work at a Pizza
  Place 2.7K (data).
- **Newest:** Kukirin Life (16.6K, created 2026-09-19) is a vehicle sim with a `roleplay` name
  tag, #9 on Up-and-Coming (data). A vehicle-first hook can still open the genre (inferred).
- **Pet Care subgenre:** 187K across 7 games; Adopt Me holds 182K (data).
- **Opportunity (inferred):** don't build a general town against Brookhaven. Build one setting
  with deep role tools (a hospital, an airport, a ferry, a theme park), or a roleplay layer on a
  new verb, as Kukirin Life did with scooters.

## Scope for a solo dev

Be honest: a full town is a content business. Brookhaven ships houses, vehicles and props every
week with a studio behind it (community, wiki 2026). Week counts below are (inferred) for one
developer with Rojo, Studio MCP and no artist.

**MVP:** one setting (for example a hospital plus 3 streets), 6–8 roles with 2–3 tools each,
role outfits as `HumanoidDescription` presets, 4 house variants on spawnable plots with a lock,
3 vehicles, about 60 props with grid placement and per-player caps, emotes, private-server
owner tools, filtered text, 3–4 passes. No currency, no trading.

| Week | Build |
|---|---|
| 1 | Map blockout sized for 16 players; spawn; role picker; outfit presets via `Humanoid:ApplyDescriptionAsync` on the server |
| 2 | Role tools that act on another player (`ProximityPrompt` + server checks): treat, cuff, serve, carry; animations and status effects |
| 3 | House plots: claim, lock, kick-from-house; prop placement with caps; filtered house names |
| 4 | Vehicles (`VehicleSeat`), 3 locations with scene cues (beds you sit on, counters, desks) |
| 5 | Private-server owner panel (`game.PrivateServerOwnerId`), block/mute, report button, passes with `ProcessReceipt`, analytics |
| 6 | Mobile pass, performance pass with 16 bots, maturity questionnaire, launch |
| Weekly after | 5–10 new props or 1 house or 1 vehicle, on a fixed day |

**Cut from v1:** a full city, jobs that pay, currency, furniture shops, trading, pets,
in-game phone texting, voice features, free building, brand events. Generate simple props with
Studio's mesh tools or build from parts; never copy another game's buildings or layout.

## Top examples

- Brookhaven (case study: brookhaven): the open sandbox at scale; weekly drops, passes
  for options, owner tools in private servers.
- Adopt Me! (case study: adopt-me): roleplay base plus pet care, collection and trading.
- Berry Avenue (no case study): sandbox roleplay with house and travel passes; 43K players
  (data, 2026-10-22).
- Welcome to Bloxburg (no case study): build-and-work life sim with build-tool passes; 18.7K
  players (data, 2026-10-22).

## Build checklist for agents

### Do

- State in the description that the game is a roleplay game, and make role tools and outfits
  the core. Never use the word "hangout" in the title or description.
- Answer the maturity questionnaire for the most extreme content; target Minimal or Mild.
- Give every role at least 2 tools that act on another player, validated on the server
  (range, role, cooldown).
- Run all player-visible custom text (house names, signs, job titles, pet names) through
  `TextService:FilterStringAsync` and show it with
  `TextFilterResult:GetNonChatStringForBroadcastAsync`.
- Route any in-game messaging (phones, radios, walkie-talkies) through `TextChatService`
  channels (`TextChannel:AddUserAsync`), never a custom RemoteEvent chat.
- Use `TextChannel:DisplaySystemMessage` and emotes for event cues, since many players can't
  chat.
- Put the lock on the house, not on single-person rooms. Make beds sit or sleep animations for
  one player and bathrooms open-plan props.
- Cap props per player and per server; give private-server owners kick, lock and prop-removal
  tools that only work in their own server.
- Keep all core houses, vehicles and roles free; sell variants, packs, VIP and convenience.
- Offer a timed free trial (rewarded ad or event try-on area) for paid houses and vehicles.
- Ship new content on a fixed weekly day and show it in a "New this week" sign at spawn.
- Test with 16 bots on a low-end phone profile before launch.

### Don't

- Don't add dating, weddings, kissing, couple emotes, or boyfriend/girlfriend roles.
- Don't build bars, clubs, nightclubs, or games set mainly in bedrooms or bathrooms.
- Don't add drawing, spray paint, whiteboards or editable pixel canvases.
- Don't let players send each other text outside `TextChatService`, or post personal info on
  signs.
- Don't let the client spawn houses, vehicles or props without server checks on ownership,
  caps and passes.
- Don't paywall the core roles, the first house, or the basic car.
- Don't use realistic blood or lethal weapons; keep arrests and fights stylized.
- Don't copy Brookhaven, Berry Avenue, Adopt Me or Bloxburg maps, house designs, names, logos
  or UI.
