# Trend lifecycle and clone waves

## In one line

Roblox trends spike on weekly updates and events, fill up with clones within weeks, and the
original usually loses 99%+ of its peak within a year. The format often survives in a new skin.
Joining one only makes sense with a real spin, a short build, and the tracker telling you where
the wave is.

## The two waves everyone copies

| | Grow a Garden | Steal a Brainrot |
|---|---|---|
| Released | 2025-03-26 (press) | 2025-05-16 (press) |
| Early size | ~1,000 players when Splitting Point bought in, 2025-04 (press) | 5M peak, 2025-07 (press) |
| Peaks | 5M+ 2025-05-18 · 11.7M 2025-05-31 · 21.3M 2025-06-21 · 22.3M 2025-08-23 (press) | 20M 2025-08 · 24M 2025-09-13 · 25.4M 2025-10 (press); 12.8M at a Bruno Mars concert, 2026-01 (press) |
| What drove each peak | Named weekly updates (Blood Moon, Bizzy Bees, Summer Update) (press) | Events: mock "admin war" with Grow a Garden 2025-08-23, "Extinct Event" 2025-09-13 (press) |
| Now (2026-10-22) | **16.9K** players (data), −99.9% from peak | **104K** players (data), −99.6% from peak |
| Visits | 35.98B (data, 2026-10-22) | 74.09B (data, 2026-10-22) |

- Peaks don't hold. Grow a Garden fell from 21.3M to 7.6M within an hour of its June 2025 peak
  (press, PocketGamer.biz via Wikipedia).
- Grow a Garden's first version took about three days to build (press, co-owner interview).
  Low build cost is why waves fill up so fast.
- Steal a Brainrot's owners filed at least four lawsuits against alleged imposters from
  2025-11 onward (press, Bloomberg 2026-03-24). Copying assets or names is a legal risk, not
  just a discovery one.

## Stages of a wave, and what each looks like in the tracker

| Stage | What you see in `private/trends/latest.md` | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Spark | One young game on Up-and-Coming / breakouts with players far above its age | Study it. Too early to know if it's a format or a one-off. |
| 2. Explosion | The original's players jump on update/event days; 1-day risers show +50–200% | Prototype the core loop in days if you want in; don't wait for certainty. |
| 3. Clone wave | 3+ rising games share a theme ("Forming clone waves"); titles copy the naming pattern | Only join with a spin (below). Newest entry under 30 days old = still being joined. |
| 4. Consolidation | The biggest game holds 60%+ of the theme's players ("one game dominates") | You're now competing with the leader's update cadence. Need a different core twist, not a reskin. |
| 5. Decay | Theme players fall on 7-day comparisons while the game count stays high ("crowded") | Too late for a straight clone. |
| 6. Mutation | A new game combines the format with another proven loop and outgrows the original | This is where late entrants win. |

All stage labels are `(inferred)` from the 2025–2026 waves above and the first tracker snapshot.

## What the tracker sees right now (data, 2026-10-22, single snapshot)

- **steal**: 9 charted games, 1.08M players; Steal An Egg holds 81%. Steal An Egg was created
  2026-07-25, 14 months after Steal a Brainrot, and is the #1 game on Roblox (877K players).
  Break and Steal an Egg (created 2026-09-05) has 69K.
- **egg**: 7 rising games, created 2026-07-25 → 2026-10-04. Still being joined.
- **"Verb a Noun" titles** (Steal An Egg, Ride a Dino, Fake an Egg, Chop A Forest, Feed A
  Mosquito): 48 charted, 17 rising. The naming pattern is a wave of its own.
- **plus-one** ("+1 ___"): 15 charted, 5 rising, all created 2026-09-18 → 2026-10-05, each
  applying "+1 per second/click/kill" to a new verb (venom, assassin leveling, pickaxe, gun forge).
  The leader, +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, was down ~45% over 30 days (community, RoWatcher
  2026-10-24), so the format is moving to new verbs rather than growing ([plus-one-stat](../genres/plus-one-stat.md)).
- Single snapshot only: re-check these after a week of history (risers need it).

## What Roblox says (official)

From the discovery docs:
- Games whose metadata and place files closely resemble existing games are no longer
  prioritized for recommendations and may rank lower in search. The example given is the same
  title and visuals as an already published game (official).
- Best practice is to "add your own spin" to trends in the title, images, description and
  in-game content, and to use original imagery and naming (official).
- Roblox reclassifies content quality with every update; reduced exposure shows as a banner in
  the Creator Dashboard (official).
- Standout Games is a hand-curated sort for novel games: distinctive mechanics, distinctive
  visuals, or underrepresented genres (official). Being different is a separate path to
  traffic.

## Why waves form so fast

- The loops are simple and explain themselves in seconds, sessions are short, and the social
  conflict (stealing, raiding) produces clips (press, on Steal a Brainrot's rise; see
  [social-and-virality](social-and-virality.md)).
- Recommendations retrieve candidates on engagement and retention signals
  ([discovery-algorithm](discovery-algorithm.md)), so a format that already retains well gets
  more chances. (inferred)
- Clones of simple games take days to build; a long-time developer reports successful
  originals getting cloned within about 12 hours (community, DevForum 2026-07-14). Roblox's
  tools and AI speed this up (press, Plagiarism Today 2026-09-17).
- Game mechanics aren't protected by copyright; only assets, code and other expression are
  (press, Plagiarism Today 2026-09-17). So clones of the *design* are legal; clones of the
  *content* are not.
- Platform pressure: Roblox reportedly lost about $70B in market value in August 2026, which
  the company attributed to a lack of viral games (press, Plagiarism Today 2026-09-17;
  unverified against Roblox's own statements).

## Is it too late? A checklist

Answer from the latest trends report. Two or more "late" answers: don't join with a reskin.
(inferred thresholds, starting values to tune)

- [ ] Is the newest rising game in the theme under 30 days old? (No = late)
- [ ] Is the leader's share of the theme's players under 80%? (No = late; you're fighting one game)
- [ ] Are theme players flat or rising on the 7-day comparison? (No = late)
- [ ] Is the theme's median game above the median charted game? (No = crowded)
- [ ] Can you ship a playable version in 4 weeks or less? (No = the wave may move on before you launch)
- [ ] Do you have a spin you can state in one sentence that changes what the player *does*? (No = don't)

Being late to the *original* isn't fatal: Steal An Egg arrived 14 months after Steal a Brainrot
and passed it, by combining stealing with egg hatching (data + inferred).

## How to add a spin

Ranked from strongest to weakest `(inferred)`:

1. **Combine two proven loops.** Steal + egg hatching (Steal An Egg). Each half is familiar; the
   mix is new.
2. **Change the verb, keep the structure.** The incremental "Verb a Noun" games keep the
   collect → upgrade → rebirth skeleton and swap the action (chop, feed, ride).
3. **Change the social shape.** PvP stealing → co-op defending, or 1v1 → teams.
4. **Change the session.** A 30-minute grind loop → 5-minute rounds, or the reverse.
5. **Change the setting/theme only.** Weakest: it's what discovery penalizes when the title and
   visuals also match.

Always make the spin visible in the icon and first 10 seconds
([first-time-experience](first-time-experience.md),
[metadata-thumbnails-icons](metadata-thumbnails-icons.md)).

## Exit plan

- Treat a wave game as a short bet: MVP in ≤ 4 weeks, then judge it on D1 and play-through
  rate in the first week ([retention](retention.md), [analytics](analytics.md)). (inferred)
- Plan an update cadence before launch. Weekly updates and events drove every peak above
  ([live-ops-and-updates](live-ops-and-updates.md)); games that stop updating fade within weeks
  (community, RoWatcher analysis, undated 2026).
- Reuse systems across bets (data saving, shops, UI kit) so the second attempt is faster
  ([player-data](../patterns/player-data.md)).
- Pay-to-win carries reputation risk with this audience: Polygon criticized Steal a Brainrot
  for keeping the best items behind payment (press, 2025-07-25).

## Checklist for agents

### Do
- Read `private/trends/latest.md` "Forming clone waves" and "Saturation estimate" before
  recommending a trend game, and cite the numbers with their date.
- State the spin in one sentence and show where it appears in the core loop, icon and title.
- Note which stage (table above) the wave is in.
- Use original names, art, sounds and UI.

### Don't
- Don't copy a trend game's title pattern *and* its visuals; that's the exact case discovery
  deprioritizes.
- Don't use another game's assets, characters, names or sounds (lawsuits are being filed).
- Don't call a wave "rising" from a single snapshot; say "on Up-and-Coming" instead.
- Don't plan a wave game with a build longer than about a month without a reason it will
  still matter at launch.
